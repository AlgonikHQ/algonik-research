## A tool worth sharing: how often does luck pass a prop challenge?

I built a simulator that replays a strategy's trades thousands of times against a 2-step prop challenge (+10%, then +5%, 5% daily loss, 10% max loss, 1% risk per trade, no time limit).

| Average per trade | Passes both phases |
|---|---|
| 0R, no edge at all | about 32% |
| +0.06R | about 65% |
| +0.12R | about 85% |

About a third of zero-edge traders pass at 1% risk just by luck. A pass on its own proves very little; repeated payouts are what count.
