## What we learned

- **Fast ideas lose to costs.** For intraday indices and pairs, the flipped trade lost just as much, so there was no direction information. The loss was all spread.
- **Slow ideas carry real information, but it's small.** Trend, carry, pre-Fed drift and positioning all beat random entries 96-100% of the time. The edge is 0.05-0.15R per trade, and on CFDs the overnight financing markup takes most of it.
- **The venue matters.** The same trend signal went from -0.08R to +0.14R just by removing the CFD financing markup.
- **Correction:** I estimated intraday index costs at 2% of a stop. The real cost was about 6-7% (0.085R per trade). Still about a third of what M15 FX costs.

## Live demo changes (cost and operations lab)

- Real fills since August cost 0.184R per round trip. Exits paid about twice the entry spread.
- In 7 of 8 wide-spread stop-outs at the 5pm New York rollover, the mid price never reached the stop: spread alone took them out. A rollover guard now widens stops for that window and restores them afterwards.
- Account rules now mirror a UK-available prop firm's swing account (5% daily loss on equity, 10% static max loss).
